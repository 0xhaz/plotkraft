Re: THE QUIET PART — coverage follow-up

Overall this is close. Some thoughts before we go to the director list.

We need more of Maya's personal life. The sister in the diner is the only glimpse of who
she is outside the job — build that out, maybe a second scene with her later. Audiences
need someone to hold onto.

The parking garage stall is a momentum killer. Cut it or compress it hard — we're
twenty minutes in with no forward motion.

Keep Gibbs. The legal detail is what makes this feel authentic and it's cheap to shoot.

Love the ending. Reeves' explanation is the most interesting idea in the script.
